# Demo video script (under 10 minutes)

1. Open the supplied PDF brief and identify the decision owner and quarter deadlines.
2. Show the brief adaptation and the synthetic data boundaries.
3. Show the source inventory and decision-changing research plan.
4. Show facts, hypotheses, forecasts, and the evidence graph.
5. Present the four materially different options and their strongest objections.
6. Present the aligned product and communication strategies.
7. Show the pilot design, guardrails, resources, and 285-million-ruble target.
8. Add a new synthetic file under `data/new/`, run `recheck_decisions.py`, and show the checkpoint.
9. State that human approval is required and no real external action is authorized.
